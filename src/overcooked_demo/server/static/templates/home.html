Participant Information Sheet and Consent Form
Collaborative Agents with LLM-based Dialogue for Human-AI Teamwork
Worktribe ethics application ID 8349664

The aim of this research is to address a key research gap by evaluating a symbolic Artificial Intelligence agent’s performance in cooperative tasks with humans, specifically within the Overcooked-AI game environment. We also investigate whether incorporating dialogue interaction through a chat can improve a user's collaborative experience. The research is conducted by Rafael C. Cardoso from the University of Aberdeen, Arabella Sinclair from University College London, Angelo Ferrando and Gianluca Scollo from the University of Modena-Reggio Emilia, and Andrea Gatti and Viviana Mascardi from the University of Genoa. The results of this research will be used for a peer- reviewed open access publication which will be made accessible through principal investigator’s website at https://www.abdn.ac.uk/people/rafael.cardoso#publications. We appreciate your interest in participating in this study. We would like to invite you to take part if you are over 18 and under 60 years of age. Please take some time to read the following information carefully before agreeing to participate (if you wish to) by ticking the consent boxes at the bottom of this page. Your participation in the survey is entirely voluntary, and you can opt out at any stage by closing and exiting the browser.

If you decide to take part, you will be asked to play multiple games with an AI agent collaborator at different levels of complexity, and then answer questions about your experience. After accepting taking part in the study, you will answer an initial pre-questionnaire with some general purpose questions: age group and sex (both with options for participants who prefer not to say), if you are native English speaker or not, as well as some questions related to your past experience with videogames and cooperative games. You will then read the instructions and play two games in two different layouts (for a total of four games), with each game lasting 60 seconds. There will be questions in between games, and a chat feature where you will be able to communicate with the AI agent. A final questionnaire will be shown at the end of the study. We will collect the chat history, questionnaire answers, and game metrics (score, etc.). No identifiable data will be collected at any point of the study. Your answers will help us to shape the design of future collaborative AI agents, as well as form a proof of concept for Large Language Models interfaces with AI agents. The study should take approximately 20 to 40 minutes to complete. Please be aware that after completing the study, you will not be able to withdraw your data.

Your answers will be treated confidentially and the information you provide will remain anonymous in any scholarly outputs. All data generated by the study will be securely stored at the University of Aberdeen and retained in accordance with the University of Aberdeen Research Data Management Policy for 5 years after the project end date. Your data can only be accessed by the principal investigator. You can also consult the University’s Privacy Notice for Research Participants for further information on how we manage your data.

The research has been approved by the Physical Sciences & Engineering Ethics Board, University of Aberdeen on the 20 May 2026. If you have any concerns about the way in which the study has been conducted, you can contact the Convener of the Physical Sciences & Engineering Ethics Board, at copsethics@abdn.ac.uk.
